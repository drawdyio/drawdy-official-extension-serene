# Serene ♪(´▽｀)

**you draw it. it sings it.**

Doodle a wave, a zigzag, a little heart, whatever you like. Hit play and a
playhead glides across your drawing, plinking a note everywhere it bumps into
your lines.

No music lessons needed, promise. Every note snaps to a scale, so even the
messiest scribble comes out sounding pretty ✧˖°

---

## let's make some noise ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the little **piano** in the extension rail on the right.
2. Hit **Create Serene frame**. Tada, a frame! It's your stage, and only the
   stuff you draw inside it gets to sing.
3. Grab the pen and go wild in there.
4. Press **▶** next to your frame in the Serene panel. Listen ♡

> (・_・;) **hearing nothing?** Your browser wants one click inside the Serene
> panel before it allows sound. Click anywhere in the panel once and you're
> all set.

---

## how does a drawing sing? (⊙_⊙)?

The frame works a bit like sheet music.

- **Left to right is time.** The playhead starts on the left and wanders
  right. Wider frame, longer song.
- **Up and down is pitch.** High lines sing high, low lines sing low.
- **Long strokes glide.** A pen line slides from note to note like someone
  humming along.
- **Dots go *plink*!** One tiny dot is one tiny note.
- **Shapes sparkle.** Rectangles, circles and diamonds play as a little run of
  notes around their edges.
- **Stack lines, get chords.** Lines sitting above each other ring out
  together.
- **Faint lines whisper.** Turn a stroke's opacity down and it plays softer.
- **You can draw mid-song!** New strokes get played when the playhead reaches
  them. Even the laser pointer joins in (ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ*:･ﾟ✧

## your frames on the board (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

Each Serene frame wears a tiny name tag on top, showing its name and how long
it plays.

- **Click the name tag** to open the Serene panel right on that frame.
- **Hover a frame** to peek at its guides: seconds along the top (`1s`,
  `2s`…) and notes down the side (`C4`, `C5`…).
- **Drag the playhead** by its little handle at the top to start from
  anywhere you like.

Want more? **+ New frame** in the panel pops a fresh one right next to your
last.

## the Serene panel ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ

### Frames

All your frames in one cozy list, each with a mini preview of its drawing.

- **▶** plays, press again to pause.
- Click a frame to fly over to it on the board.
- Double-click a name to give it a new one.

### Scale

The mood of the whole thing. Rest your cursor on one for a sec to hear a
little taste.

| Scale | Vibe |
| --- | --- |
| Major pentatonic | bright, zero wrong notes (start here!) |
| Major | happy and familiar |
| Dorian | cool and a little jazzy |
| Lydian | dreamy and floaty |
| Mixolydian | bluesy and laid-back |
| Hirajoshi | calm, Japanese-inspired |

### Range

How low and how high your drawing reaches. Slide the two white handles on the
mini keyboard: the left one is the bottom of the frame, the right one is the
top. They play their note as you slide ♫

### Backing

Bring in a little band behind your melody (ﾉ´ヮ`)ﾉ*:･ﾟ✧

- **Off**: just you and your doodle
- **Pop**: bouncy and singalong-y
- **Classic**: gentle and timeless
- **Simple**: two chords swaying back and forth
- **Drone**: one warm, cozy chord that just hums along

Rest your cursor on a style to hear a sneak preview. Greyed-out ones aren't
available in that scale.

Then dress it up:

- **Voicing** goes from just a deep bass note (**Bass**) all the way to big,
  full chords (**Full**).
- **Rhythm** plays each chord once (**1×**), twice (**2×**), four times
  (**4×**), or rolls through it like a harp going up (**Arp ↑**) or down
  (**Arp ↓**).

### Feel

Tap **Feel** to open the fine-tuning sliders.

- **Speed**: how fast the playhead strolls
- **Attack**: how notes start, low for crisp little plucks, high for soft
  swells
- **Notes**: how loud your drawing is
- **Backing**: how loud the band is (shows up when a backing is on)
- **Reverb**: from cozy bedroom to giant cathedral

Double-click any slider to snap it back to its default. Serene remembers your
settings for next time, too (´｡• ᵕ •｡`)

## little things to try (っ˘ω˘ς )

- One slow, lazy wave across the whole frame. Instant lullaby.
- Dots sprinkled up and down for a music box feel.
- A staircase going up, and listen to it climb.
- **Hirajoshi** + **Drone**, then draw veeery slowly. Zen garden mode.
- **Pop** + **Arp ↑**, then scribble like nobody's watching.
