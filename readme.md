# Serene ♪(´▽｀)

**you draw it. it sings it.**

Doodle anything, press play, and listen to your drawing turn into a little
song. Higher lines sing higher notes, and every note lands on a pretty
scale, so nothing ever sounds wrong ✧˖°

---

## let's play ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **piano** in the extension rail.
2. Hit **Create Serene frame**.
3. Draw anything inside the frame.
4. Press **▶** in the Serene panel and listen ♡ Turn on the loop button to make it keeps going

> (・_・;) **no sound?** Click once anywhere in the Serene panel, then try
> again.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Scale**: the mood. Bright, dreamy, jazzy, bluesy, calm… pick a vibe.
- **Range**: how low and how high your drawing can go.
- **Backing**: add a little band behind your melody: Pop, Classic, Simple or
  Drone. Try the arpeggios under **Rhythm** ♫
- **Feel**: speed, softness, volume and echo.

---

## little things to try (っ˘ω˘ς )

- One slow wave across the frame. Instant lullaby.
- Sprinkle dots for a music box.
- **Hirajoshi** + **Drone**, then draw veeery slowly.
- **Pop** + **Arp ↑**, then scribble like nobody's watching.

Have fun and happy doodling ♪～(´ε｀ )
